RReadyIQ
A new product line for IDIQ

See if a borrower qualifies. Build their credit if they don’t.

Mortgage companies and real estate agents run a soft credit check for $14.95 — the same FICO scores mortgage underwriting uses, with no hard inquiry. If they qualify, pull the full report. If they don’t, we report the rent and bills they already pay to all three bureaus until they do.

Not one of those people was declined. A decline is a decision on a file, and none of these got that far. An officer or an agent looked at a score, did the arithmetic in their head, decided it was not worth pulling, and quietly stopped calling. There is no record of it anywhere, which is exactly why nobody ever goes back — and why the number is bigger than any decline report will ever show you.

Five ideas

Everything we have talked about folds into these five. Each one is written the same way — who it is for, when we could actually do it, who pays, what the consumer gets, how it runs, what it means for a lender and for an agent, what is good about it, what is wrong with it, and the smallest pilot that would settle the argument.

North starOne simple program, not a mortgage CRM. The consumer credit-building, dispute, monitoring and reporting products are the value; links, alerts, CRM connections and the workspace are only how they get delivered. A lender, loan officer or agent introduces somebody, that person checks their own credit, works on it, and asks to be reviewed again.
What we can do now, and what would take work
  1. We can do this now — the consumer products already exist and already do this. Links, a short form and a CSV upload are not development. Four of the five ideas sit here.
  2. Needs a small build — writing statuses back into a CRM, and getting alerts to a loan officer rather than only to the consumer. Worth doing once a pilot has produced something to point at.
  3. Needs real development — sitting inside Encompass or Blend, a lead provider API, a full workspace. Listed so it is on the record, but it should wait.
